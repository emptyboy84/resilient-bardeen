ABOUT US

## Bringing technologyinto everyday life.
We want more people to benefit from AI and data without having to navigate their complexity. Our work is to build products that make new technology useful in familiar tasks.
Our current focus is the creator economy. We apply AI and data to the problems people encounter when finding collaborators, understanding each other, and working together.
HOW WE WORK

## How we work together
01

### Trust our colleagues.
We respect each other's responsibilities and judgment. That trust lets us keep unnecessary rules and restrictions to a minimum.
02

### Discuss things openly.
We share our reasoning and question ideas to reach better decisions. We focus on the problem and possible solutions.
03

### Commit to the decision.
Once we decide together, we work in the same direction. Each of us takes responsibility for helping that decision succeed.
