**NomadHer: Solo Female Travel** is available on the Apple App Store for iPhone and iPad[1]. 

### **About the App**
* **Developer:** NomadHer Inc.[1]
* **Purpose:** A global community and safety-focused application designed for female globetrotters to encourage and empower safe solo travel[2].
* **Key Features:**
  * **Global Community Feed:** Share travel stories, ask questions, exchange tips, and address worries about solo traveling with women worldwide[2].
  * **Local Search & Messenger:** Connect with nearby female travelers or local women to find travel buddies and make friends on the road[2][3].
  * **Events & Workshops:** Participate in online and offline events, festivals, and workshops tailored for female travelers[2].
  * **Solo Travel Guidebook:** Access curated guides and essential resources created in collaboration with fellow community members (*NomadHers*)[2].
  * **Safety & Privacy:** Employs registration management and a community built on mutual respect to protect user privacy and security[2].
